Simplify Money — Product Track Flow Teardown

Author: Dipanshu Raj  |  Feature: "Track" Flow (Automated Financial Ledger Synchronization)  |  Repository: github.com/RjDipanshu/ledger-sync-seed

1. Executive Summary

Simplify Money's core value proposition answers the fundamental question: "Where did my money go?" without forcing users to manually enter transactions. The "Track" flow serves as the operational engine of this experience. It ingests semi-structured notification streams (SMS, notification listener, bank alert emails), normalizes them into immutable transactional ledger records, rolls up micro-expenses, and maintains running balances across multiple accounts.

2. End-to-End Architecture & Ingestion Journey

[SMS / Notification Received] │ ▼ [On-Device Filter & Noise Rejection] ──(Drop)──► Ignored: Non-Txn (OTPs, Loan Promos, Courier Spam) │ (Settled Bank Alert) ▼ [Multi-Channel Deduplication Engine] ──► Merges SMS + Email alerts for same transaction │ (Correlation Key: {Account} : {MinuteBucket} : {Amount} : {Direction}) ▼ [Regex & Bank-Format Parsers] ────────► HDFC SMS, ICICI SMS V1/V2, Email RFC 1123, E-Mandates │ ▼ [Four-Category Classifier] ────────────► SPEND, INCOME, MICRO (≤ ₹100), TRANSFER (Inter-Account) │ ▼ [Immutable Ledger Store] ──────────────► Amazon DynamoDB Single-Table / SQL Ledger

Stage-by-Stage Breakdown:

  • Stage 1: Permission Priming & User Trust: Financial tracking requires Android SMS permissions. The app primes users with clear visual cards explaining that banking OTPs and personal messages are strictly ignored.
  • Stage 2: Filter & Ingestion: Out of 522 raw messages in Corpus-A, 41 non-transactional messages (promotional loan offers, ATM OTPs, delivery updates) are safely filtered without entering the ledger.
  • Stage 3: Multi-Message Deduplication: Banks issue multiple alerts (SMS, Email, UPI app push) for a single payment. Deduplication merges these using {account_last4}:{minute_bucket}:{amount}:{direction}, consolidating source_message_ids.
  • Stage 4: Categorization:
    • MICRO: UPI debits ≤ ₹100 are grouped as rollups to prevent visual clutter in the main feed.
    • TRANSFER: Intra-user money movements between known accounts are isolated to prevent inflating spend and income.
    • SPEND & INCOME: Real outflows and inflows accurately reflecting net worth shifts.

3. Identified Friction Points & Engineering Resolutions

Friction Point Observed Manifestation Engineering Resolution
Whole-Rupee Regex Trap (INC-2026-09-11) Whole-rupee alerts (Rs.5) failed two-decimal regex and erroneously captured Avl Bal: Rs.92,213.10. Updated amount regex to (?:Rs\.?|INR)\s*([0-9,]+(?:\.[0-9]{2})?) with strict trailing punctuation lookaheads.
Silent Bank SMS Drops Bank network outage or dropped SMS leads to unaccounted balance drops (e.g. ₹7,500 gap on Account 4821). Compare consecutive stated balances to identify gaps; flag discrepancies in reconciliation.json.
Notification Timing Latency SMS received minutes or hours late due to roaming or network latency. Parse transaction timestamp inside message body rather than relying on phone reception time (received_at).

4. Architectural Recommendations for Scale

  1. On-Device Hybrid Parsing vs. Cloud Processing: Parsing bank regex patterns on-device protects user privacy by never sending raw message text or sensitive numbers to cloud servers. Only normalized transaction payloads are synchronized.
  2. Reconciliation as a First-Class Feature: Rather than hiding unexplained discrepancies, surface them cleanly to users: "We noticed a ₹7,500 withdrawal on 29 July not captured via SMS. Would you like to tag this as Cash Withdrawal?"
  3. DynamoDB Single-Table Design: Partitioning transactions by ACC#<last4>#<YYYY-MM> provides guaranteed $O(1)$ monthly statement reads with zero table scans. Category summaries are pre-aggregated on a single summary item (ScannedCount = 1, Count = 1).
Conclusion
The Track flow is the foundation of user retention in personal finance. Handling edge cases, multi-message deduplication, and honest balance reconciliation ensures the ledger remains trustworthy and transparent.
